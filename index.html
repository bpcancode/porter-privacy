Doko: A Himalayan lifestyle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Doko is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or usage data.

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • The game asks for no Android permissions and does not connect to the internet.
  • There are no ads, analytics, crash reporters or third-party SDKs.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Google Play may collect information when you download the app, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
